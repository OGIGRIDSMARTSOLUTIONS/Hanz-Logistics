\section*{The Hanz Operations Standard}
\vspace{1pt}
\steprow{1}{PLAN}{We map urgency, dimensions, weight, handling requirements, route, and contingencies before tender. A documented movement plan confirms responsibilities at every handoff.}
\steprow{2}{TRACK}{Active milestones and human oversight keep the shipment visible in motion. Operators monitor exceptions and coordinate directly across carriers and ground partners.}
\steprow{3}{CLOSE OUT}{Delivery confirmation, proof-of-delivery verification, and chain of custody documentation consolidate the complete shipment record for review and compliance.}
