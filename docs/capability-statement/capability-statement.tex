\section*{The Hanz Operations Standard}
\vspace{1pt}
\steprow{1}{PLAN}{Urgency, dimensions, weight, handling needs, route, and contingencies are mapped before tender, with a documented plan for every handoff.}
\steprow{2}{TRACK}{Active milestones and human oversight keep the shipment visible in motion, with operators coordinating exceptions across carriers and partners.}
\steprow{3}{CLOSE OUT}{Delivery confirmation, proof of delivery, and chain of custody records consolidate the complete shipment file for review and compliance.}
